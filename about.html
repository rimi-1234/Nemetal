Our Story

Building Bangladesh,
One Material at a Time

Easycon BD was founded with a simple mission: to make quality construction materials accessible to everyone in Bangladesh. From small homeowners to large construction firms, we serve all with premium products at competitive prices.

Located in the heart of Mirpur-10, Dhaka, we are a one-stop destination for all your building needs — from cement and steel rods to tiles, paints, doors, and electrical materials.
