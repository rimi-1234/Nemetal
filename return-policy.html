Return Eligibility

We accept returns within 7 days of delivery. To be eligible for a return, items must be:

  • In original, unused condition
  • In original packaging with all tags intact
  • Accompanied by the original receipt or order confirmation
  • Not on the non-returnable items list

Non-Returnable Items

  • Cement, sand, and aggregate products once opened
  • Custom-cut or made-to-order products
  • Products damaged due to misuse
  • Items without original packaging
  • Electrical items once installed

Damaged or Defective Products

If you receive a damaged or defective product, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the damage. We will arrange replacement or full refund at no cost to you.

Refund Process

Once we receive and inspect the returned item, we will process your refund within 3-5 business days. Refunds are credited back to the original payment method. For COD orders, refund will be via bKash or bank transfer.

Return Shipping

For defective or wrong items, we cover the return shipping cost. For other returns (e.g., change of mind), the customer bears the return shipping cost.

How to Initiate a Return

To initiate a return, please:

  • Log in to your account and go to Order History
  • Select the order and click "Return Item"
  • Or contact us at 01955-008864 or kmmehedy@easyconbd.com
