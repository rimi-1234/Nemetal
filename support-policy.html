Our Support Commitment

At Easycon BD, we are committed to providing exceptional customer support. Our team of construction material experts is ready to assist you with product selection, order issues, and technical queries.

Response Times

  • Phone calls: Answered within business hours (Mon-Sat, 9am-6pm)
  • WhatsApp messages: Response within 2-3 hours during business hours
  • Email queries: Response within 24 business hours
  • Order issues: Resolved within 48 hours

Types of Support We Provide

  • Product information and selection guidance
  • Order placement assistance
  • Order tracking and delivery updates
  • Returns and refund processing
  • Technical queries about construction materials
  • Bulk purchase consultation
  • Warranty claims assistance

FAQ

Q: How can I cancel my order?

A: Orders can be cancelled within 2 hours of placement. Contact us immediately via phone or WhatsApp.

Q: I received wrong items. What should I do?

A: Contact us within 48 hours with photos. We will arrange pickup and replacement at no cost.

Q: How do I track my order?

A: Visit our Track Order page or contact us with your Order ID.

Q: Can I modify my order?

A: Order modifications are possible before shipping. Contact us as soon as possible.

Escalation Policy

If your issue is not resolved within the stated timeframes, please escalate by emailing kmmehedy@easyconbd.com with subject "ESCALATION: [Your Order ID]". Senior management will respond within 4 business hours.
